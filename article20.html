Article 20

Freedom of peaceful assembly and association.

Article 20 of the UDHR

Everyone has the right to freedom of peaceful assembly and association.


No one may be compelled to belong to an association.

What does this mean?

Article 20 means people have the freedom to peacefully gather with others, create groups, join organisations and choose whether they want to belong to them.

🤝 Peaceful Assembly

People can peacefully gather together.

🏛️ Association

People can create or join organisations and groups.

🔓 Freedom of Choice

People cannot be forced to belong to an association.
