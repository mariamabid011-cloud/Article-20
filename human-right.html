What Are Human Rights?

Rights that belong to every person.

Human rights are basic freedoms and protections that belong to every person simply because they are human.

🌍 Universal

Human rights belong to everyone, everywhere, regardless of who they are.

⚖️ Equal

Every person deserves dignity, fairness and equal treatment.

🕊️ Freedom

Human rights protect important freedoms that allow people to make choices.

🛡️ Protection

Rights help protect people from unfair treatment and abuse.
