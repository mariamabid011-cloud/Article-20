Article 20 in Real Life

How this right appears in everyday situations.

🏫 At School

Students can create clubs and organisations around shared interests.

🏘️ In Communities

Neighbours can work together to solve local problems.

📣 Peaceful Demonstrations

People can peacefully gather to raise awareness about important issues.

💻 Online Communities

People can connect with others who share their interests and goals.
