Why Does Article 20 Matter?

People are stronger when they can work together.

Article 20 allows people to connect with others who share their interests, concerns or goals.

📢 Share Ideas

People can discuss ideas and issues that matter to them.

🤝 Build Communities

Groups allow people to support one another.

✊ Create Change

Peaceful collective action can raise awareness and encourage positive change.

A community can often achieve things that one person cannot achieve alone.
