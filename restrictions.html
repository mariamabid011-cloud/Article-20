When Rights Are Restricted

Why freedom of assembly and association matters.

When people cannot peacefully gather or form organisations, they may lose important ways to express themselves and participate in society.

🚫 Less Participation

People may have fewer opportunities to participate in their communities.

🔇 Less Expression

People may struggle to share ideas and concerns collectively.

🧩 Weaker Communities

Groups and organisations may become harder to create and maintain.

Can a society truly be free if people are not free to peacefully come together?
