Why Do Humans Need Rights?

Rights protect our dignity, freedom and voices.

Human rights help people live safely, freely and with dignity. They also allow people to participate in society and express themselves.

🗣️ Our Voices

People should be able to express their ideas and opinions peacefully.

🤝 Our Communities

Rights allow people to work together and support their communities.

⚖️ Equality

Rights help people receive fair and respectful treatment.

🛡️ Safety

Human rights help protect people from unfair treatment and abuse.
