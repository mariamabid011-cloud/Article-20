Article 20

Freedom of Peaceful Assembly and Association

Everyone has the right to freedom of peaceful assembly and association.

No one may be compelled to belong to an association.

In simple words, people have the freedom to peacefully gather, create groups, join organisations and choose whether they want to belong to them.

Why Does Article 20 Matter?

Positive change often begins when people come together.

📢

🤝

✊

What If This Right Is Restricted?

When people cannot peacefully gather or form organisations, they may lose important ways to express themselves and participate in society.


Less Participation

People may have fewer opportunities to participate in their communities.

Less Expression

People may struggle to share ideas and concerns collectively.

Less Community

Groups and organisations can become harder to create or maintain.

Think About It.

Can a society truly be free if people are not free to peacefully come together?
