Know Your Rights.

Understanding is the first step toward positive change.

① Learn

Understand your rights and the rights of others.

② Question

Think critically about fairness, freedom and equality.

③ Discuss

Talk respectfully about important issues.

④ Participate

Take part in positive and peaceful community activities.

⑤ Respect

Respect the dignity and rights of other people.

Rights belong to everyone.

Know them. Respect them. Stand up for them peacefully.
